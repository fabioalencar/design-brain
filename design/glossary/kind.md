---
type: Term
title: Kind (harvested / heuristic / bias)
---
What a candidate is made of. `harvested` (default) comes from the designer's own projects and carries transcript, DDR, audit, or repo evidence. `heuristic` is a published usability heuristic adopted as a standing check. `bias` is a cognitive bias the interface must not exploit, or one the designer must not fall into, recorded as something to avoid. Heuristics and biases cite a `reference:` source and compile into their own skill sections (DDR-006).

## Rule

A struck-through "was" price, a "compare at" figure or a monthly figure next to an annual one reflects a price that was actually charged or is actually available.

Price per unit is not cherry-picked to shrink the number.

## Why

The first number seen sets the scale for every judgement after it; a fabricated anchor makes an ordinary price feel like a bargain.

## Examples

- "R$ 79, was R$ 99" only when R$ 99 was the list price for a stated period.
- Annual plan shown as its yearly total, with the per-month equivalent secondary.

## Exceptions

Showing a real higher tier first to frame value is not deceptive when the tier is real.

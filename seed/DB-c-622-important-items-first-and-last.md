## Rule

In menus, navigation bars and lists, the items that matter most sit at the ends; the middle holds the least critical.

Do not bury a key destination among many siblings.

## Why

People recall the first and last items of a sequence best and the middle worst.

## Examples

- Mobile tab bar with Home first and Profile last, utility items between.
- A landing page that leads with the core message and closes with the call to action.

## Exceptions

Sorted data lists (alphabetical, by date) follow their sort, not this rule.

## Rule

When presenting products, plans or settings, show a short set with a recommended default and make the rest reachable by filtering or expanding.

Options are ordered and differentiated, never a flat wall of equals.

## Why

Beyond a few options, satisfaction and completion drop; people defer or abandon rather than compare.

## Examples

- Three pricing plans with one marked as the fit for most users.
- A template gallery that opens on six curated picks with "Browse all".

## Exceptions

Catalogues where the user's job is to browse widely; there, sorting and filtering do the curation.

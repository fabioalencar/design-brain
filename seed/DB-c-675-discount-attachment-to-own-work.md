## Rule

A component, pattern or page you built is valued by the same evidence as one you did not: test results, fit to the rule set, cost to maintain.

When comparing your work with a library or a colleague's, ask someone without a stake to judge, or set the criteria before looking.

## Why

Making something raises its perceived worth to the maker; the effort is felt as quality.

## Examples

- Replacing a hand-rolled date picker with the design system's after a side-by-side task test.
- Treating an accepted third-party pattern as the default unless the custom one proves better.

## Exceptions

Where the custom build encodes a confirmed rule the library cannot, keep it and record why.

## Rule

The option to say no is worded as plainly as the option to say yes: "No thanks", "Not now", "Close".

No "No, I don't want to save money", no mocking, no emotional framing on the refusal.

## Why

Guilt-laden decline copy pressures the user through shame rather than informing them, and reads as contempt.

## Examples

- Newsletter modal: "Subscribe" / "No thanks".
- Upgrade prompt: "Upgrade" / "Stay on the free plan".

## Exceptions

None.

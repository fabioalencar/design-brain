## Rule

Layouts use a small number of aligned, regular forms; overlapping, ambiguous or ornate arrangements are avoided.

If a viewer can misread the structure, the structure is wrong.

## Why

People perceive complex images as the simplest form they can; if that simplest form is not the intended one, they see something else.

## Examples

- Cards on a uniform grid with equal gutters.
- Icons built from a few strokes on a shared grid rather than detailed illustrations.

## Exceptions

Editorial or brand moments may break regularity deliberately when the content is not task-critical.

## Rule

When the user dismisses a request (notifications, rating, upgrade, app install), the product remembers and does not ask again in that session, and rarely thereafter. Repeat prompts are capped, spaced and offer a permanent "don't ask again".

## Why

Repetition wears people down into a yes they did not mean, and every reappearance costs attention and goodwill.

## Examples

- A "rate this app" prompt shown once after a successful task, with "Never".
- A push-permission pre-prompt shown only at a relevant moment, not on launch.

## Exceptions

Safety and legal notices that must be acknowledged may persist, folded rather than modal.

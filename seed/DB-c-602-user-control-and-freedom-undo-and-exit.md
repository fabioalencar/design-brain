## Rule

Every flow has a visible way out, and every consequential action can be undone or cancelled. Mistaken actions are recoverable without a support ticket.

## Why

Users act by trial; when the cost of a wrong click is low they explore confidently and recover fast. Locked-in paths create anxiety and abandonment.

## Examples

- A dialog with an explicit "Cancel" and an Escape key that closes it without saving.
- A delete that shows "Item removed. Undo" for a few seconds before committing.

## Exceptions

Truly irreversible actions (sending money, sending a message) use confirmation before rather than undo after.

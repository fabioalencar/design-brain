## Rule

The basket contains only what the user chose.

Insurance, warranties, tips, round-ups and bundled extras are opt-in, unchecked by default, and removable in one click.

## Why

Users trust the basket to reflect their decisions; a smuggled line item is a small theft that costs the whole checkout its credibility.

## Examples

- "Add protection plan" as an unchecked option with a visible price.
- A tip selector with no preselected amount.

## Exceptions

None.

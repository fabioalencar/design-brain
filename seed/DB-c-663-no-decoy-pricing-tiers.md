## Rule

A pricing or plan table does not include an option whose only purpose is to make a neighbour look better. Each tier is a real fit for a real segment, and the differences between tiers are stated in the user's terms.

## Why

A dominated decoy shifts choice toward the target by comparison rather than by need; it is a manipulation of judgment, not information.

## Examples

- Three plans where each has a customer it genuinely serves, with a plain comparison table.
- Removing a "Medium" tier that nobody buys and that exists only to sell "Large".

## Exceptions

None.

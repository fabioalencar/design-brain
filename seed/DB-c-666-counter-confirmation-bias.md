## Rule

Research tasks, review checklists and success criteria are written before seeing results and include the ways the design could be wrong.

Sessions are observed for failures, quotes that contradict the hypothesis are recorded, and the reviewer states what would change their mind.

## Why

Once a direction is chosen, attention filters for support; unstructured research becomes a search for permission.

## Examples

- Usability tasks framed neutrally ("find the invoice for March"), not as leading questions.
- A design review that starts by listing three things that would make the direction wrong.

## Exceptions

None; the bias is universal and only counteracted by process.

## Rule

One concept has one name, one control and one location across the product, and the product follows the platform's conventions unless there is a documented reason not to.

Internal consistency and platform consistency are both checked.

## Why

Users learn a rule once and expect it to hold; every exception must be re-learned and is read as a bug or a trick.

## Examples

- "Delete" is never "Remove" on one screen and "Trash" on another.
- Primary actions sit in the same corner of every dialog.

## Exceptions

A deliberate, documented deviation from platform convention that serves a measured need.

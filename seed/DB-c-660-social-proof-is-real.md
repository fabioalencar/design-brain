## Rule

Testimonials, ratings, purchase notifications and user counts come from real, identifiable data.

No stock-photo customers, no generated reviews, no "someone in Lisbon just bought" toasts without a live event behind them.

## Why

Social proof works because people assume it is true; fabricating it is fraud dressed as UI.

## Examples

- Reviews with verified-purchase marks and dates.
- Logo strip showing only clients who agreed to appear.

## Exceptions

Illustrative placeholders in an unpublished prototype, clearly marked as such.

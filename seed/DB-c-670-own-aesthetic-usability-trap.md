## Rule

When reviewing your own or a peer's work, separate the two questions: does it look right, and does it work?

Assess the second with a task walk-through or a test, not a glance. A beautiful mockup is a hypothesis about usability, not evidence of it.

## Why

Attractive designs feel easier to use, to designers as much as to users, so flaws are forgiven at the exact moment they are cheapest to fix.

## Examples

- A review that walks the primary task step by step before discussing the visual layer.
- Testing a greybox version of a screen the team already loves.

## Exceptions

None.

## Rule

Actions, options and the context needed to decide are visible or one obvious step away.

The user should not have to remember a value from a previous screen, a hidden gesture or a menu path.

## Why

Recognising a visible cue is far cheaper than retrieving it from memory; hidden affordances go unused.

## Examples

- A search field that shows recent queries and suggested filters.
- A form that displays the previously entered address on the confirmation step instead of asking the user to remember it.

## Exceptions

Rarely used advanced controls may live behind a clearly labelled disclosure.

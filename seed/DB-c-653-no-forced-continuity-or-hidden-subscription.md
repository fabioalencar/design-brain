## Rule

Free trials and introductory prices state, at sign-up and on the receipt, when and how much the user will be charged next, and send a reminder before the first paid charge.

Recurring billing is never buried in terms.

## Why

Silent conversion relies on inattention; the charge lands as a surprise and the relationship is defined by the refund request.

## Examples

- Checkout summary: "Free until 12 Oct, then R$ 49/month. Cancel any time in Settings."
- An email three days before the trial ends.

## Exceptions

None.

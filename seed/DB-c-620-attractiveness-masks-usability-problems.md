## Rule

When evaluating a polished design, weigh observed task failures over stated satisfaction.

Test rough versions early, and in reviews of finished work look for problems the participants forgave.

## Why

People rate attractive interfaces as easier to use and tolerate their flaws; the flaws remain and surface later at scale.

## Examples

- A usability session where participants praise the look yet fail the checkout twice: the failure is the finding.
- Testing a wireframe before the visual layer so feedback targets the flow.

## Exceptions

None; the effect is real and also a legitimate reason to invest in polish once the flow works.

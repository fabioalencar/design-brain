## Rule

Messaging states what the user gains or what actually changes.

It does not frame declining as losing a benefit they never had, invent "missing out", or describe a downgrade as forfeiting possessions.

## Why

Losses weigh more than equal gains, so loss framing pushes harder than the facts warrant; copy that leans on it is manipulating rather than informing.

## Examples

- "Upgrade to get 100 GB" rather than "Don't lose your files".
- "Your trial ends Friday; here is what changes" rather than "You'll lose everything".

## Exceptions

Real, imminent loss (data deletion after a deadline) must be stated plainly, as fact, with the date.

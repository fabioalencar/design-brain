## Rule

Before adopting a pattern because a successful product uses it, check whether the pattern caused the success, whether products that failed used it too, and whether the borrowing context matches. Visible winners are a biased sample.

## Why

Only survivors are studied; their features are credited with outcomes that had other causes, and the failures using the same feature are invisible.

## Examples

- Not adopting a bottom sheet because a leading app has one, without testing it in the current product.
- Reviewing competitors that died alongside those that thrived.

## Exceptions

Conventions with broad adoption across winners and losers alike (see Jakob's law) are safe to follow.

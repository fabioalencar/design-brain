## Rule

Multi-step tasks display which steps are done and which remain, and interrupted work is preserved and surfaced on return. Incomplete state is visible, not lost.

## Why

Unfinished tasks stay active in memory; a visible reminder of what remains turns that tension into completion.

## Examples

- A profile setup card showing "3 of 5 steps complete".
- A draft banner: "You have an unsent message from yesterday".

## Exceptions

Do not manufacture incompleteness (fake checklists) to nag users; see the bias entries.

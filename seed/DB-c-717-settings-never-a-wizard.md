## Rule

Settings are visited to change one thing, so every setting is reachable in at most two clicks from the settings entry point and the whole group is visible at once.

Wizards are for one-time, ordered tasks (initial setup, import); after setup, the same values are exposed as ordinary settings.

## Why

Inferred from the references: NN/g defines wizards as for infrequent, sequential tasks and warns against forcing them on expert or repeat use; Apple asks that settings be quick to find and change.

## Examples

- NN/g — wizards suit rare complex tasks; do not use them where people need to jump directly to one option — nngroup.com/articles/wizards/

## Exceptions

None recorded.

## Rule

"I would understand this" is not evidence.

Decisions about comprehension, discoverability and preference are checked against people from the target group, and the team's own fluency with the product is treated as a distortion to correct for.

## Why

People overestimate how many others share their knowledge and taste; designers, who know the product, overestimate most.

## Examples

- Testing an icon-only toolbar with newcomers before shipping it without labels.
- Writing onboarding copy from a support-ticket vocabulary, not the team's.

## Exceptions

When the designer is a member of the target group, their reaction is one data point, still not the sample.

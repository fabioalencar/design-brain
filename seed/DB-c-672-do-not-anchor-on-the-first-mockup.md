## Rule

The first sketch, screenshot or AI-generated layout shown in a project is not the baseline everything else is judged against.

Produce several distinct options before iterating, and show stakeholders alternatives side by side rather than one candidate to approve.

## Why

The first concrete thing seen sets the scale for every later judgement; subsequent work becomes adjustments to it rather than independent options.

## Examples

- A kickoff that opens with three layouts, not one.
- Deferring visual polish until the structure has been chosen among alternatives.

## Exceptions

A mandated, approved direction (client brand system) is a legitimate anchor.

## Rule

Common patterns (navigation placement, cart icon, form layout, link styling) follow what users meet on the sites they spend most of their time on.

Invent only where the convention fails the task, and then keep the familiar parts intact.

## Why

Users arrive with a mental model from everywhere else; matching it means zero learning cost.

## Examples

- Logo top-left linking home; account and cart top-right.
- A search box that looks like a search box, with a magnifier icon and placeholder text.

## Exceptions

A convention that is itself a manipulative pattern is not adopted just because it is common.

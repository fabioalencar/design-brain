## Rule

A timer, "only N left", "M people are viewing" or "offer ends soon" appears only when it is true, verifiable and tied to a real constraint.

No resetting countdowns, no invented stock levels, no urgency copy on evergreen offers.

## Why

These devices exploit loss aversion and time pressure to short-circuit deliberation; once discovered, they destroy trust in every other number on the page.

## Examples

- A sale countdown that ends when the sale ends and does not reappear on reload.
- A stock indicator driven by inventory, shown only under a real threshold.

## Exceptions

Genuine deadlines (event start, ticket allocation) may be shown as absolute dates and times.

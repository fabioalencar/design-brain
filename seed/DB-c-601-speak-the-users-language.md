## Rule

Labels, messages and groupings use the words and order the user already knows from their domain, not internal names, database fields or engineering shorthand. Information appears in the sequence the real-world task follows.

## Why

Users map the screen onto what they already know; every unfamiliar term or unnatural order adds a translation step and a chance of error.

## Examples

- A checkout shows "Shipping address" before "Payment", matching the order a shop clerk would ask.
- A filter reads "Overdue invoices", not "status=3".

## Exceptions

Expert tools may adopt the community's own jargon when that jargon is the user's language.

## Rule

Remove or demote anything that does not serve the current task. Decoration, secondary information and rarely used controls are cut or folded so the primary content and action carry the screen.

## Why

Attention is finite; each extra element dilutes the visibility of the ones the user needs.

## Examples

- A settings page that shows the ten settings people change, with the rest under "Advanced".
- An article page with no sidebar competing with the text.

## Exceptions

Dense professional tools may show more at once when the user's task is comparison across many values.

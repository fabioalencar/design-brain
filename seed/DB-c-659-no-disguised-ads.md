## Rule

Paid placements are visibly marked as such and never mimic a download button, a navigation item, a result or a piece of editorial content.

Sponsored results carry a label the reader sees before they click.

## Why

An ad disguised as content earns a click by deception and teaches the user to distrust every real control on the page.

## Examples

- A "Sponsored" tag on promoted listings, in the same place on every card.
- Ad slots visually separated from the article body.

## Exceptions

None.

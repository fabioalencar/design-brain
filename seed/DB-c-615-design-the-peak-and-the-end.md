## Rule

Identify the most intense moment and the final moment of each journey and polish those first: the success screen, the confirmation, the moment an error strikes.

Do not spend the polish budget evenly.

## Why

People judge an experience by its peak and its end, not by its average; a good ending redeems a rough middle, a bad ending spoils a smooth one.

## Examples

- An order confirmation that shows what happens next and when, rather than a bare "Thank you".
- A failed payment handled gracefully, since that is the peak most users remember.

## Exceptions

None known.

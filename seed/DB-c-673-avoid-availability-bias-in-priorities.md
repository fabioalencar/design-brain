## Rule

Roadmap and fix priorities are set from counted evidence (support volume, analytics, test findings across sessions), not from the complaint that is loudest or freshest in memory.

A vivid anecdote earns an investigation, not a slot.

## Why

What comes to mind easily feels common; a recent, emotional or executive-sourced example crowds out the quieter majority.

## Examples

- A triage ritual that tallies tickets by theme before ranking.
- Recording every test failure in a shared sheet so the memorable one does not dominate.

## Exceptions

A single report of a safety, security or accessibility failure is enough to act.
